# Planned series — Cygnus Financial AI Research
**Public editorial roadmap · November 2026**

This publication studies **AI models applied to private-credit and financial-evidence tasks**. Topics below are planned research questions, not assertions of performance or announcements of confidential experiments.

## Series 1 — Financial reasoning
**Working title:** Can AI models calculate financial obligations reliably?
- Possible tasks: loan amortization, interest accrual, LTV, DSCR, cash-flow reconciliation and currency/rounding.
- Public measures after approval: independently scored correctness, unsupported financial claims, incomplete responses and uncertainty.
- Required caveats: synthetic examples are not actual underwriting.

## Series 2 — Evidence, ambiguity and abstention
**Working title:** When should a financial AI model refuse to answer?
- Questions: What happens with contradictory sources, stale values, missing inputs and ambiguous formats?
- Public measures after approval: justified abstentions, false supported assertions, unsupported citations and error categories.
- Never disclose proprietary FIP/TCIB cases, their answer keys or internal safety guard implementation.

## Series 3 — Structured outputs and operational reliability
**Working title:** Why isn't valid JSON enough in financial AI?
- Questions: Claim identifiers, source attribution, output cutoffs, incomplete reasoning and response-contract failures.
- Explain the difference between parseability, factual correctness and safety without exposing internal prompts.

## Series 4 — Model efficiency
**Working title:** The cost of reasoning in financial workflows.
- Questions: response latency, input/output/reasoning token usage, caching and practical budget limits.
- Public measures after approval: dated measured token usage and pricing sources, carefully distinguished from confirmed billing.

## Series 5 — Transparent model comparisons
**Working title:** Which model behaviors matter in private-credit intelligence?
- Future paired comparisons across models and workloads, with prespecified case selection, denominator accounting, uncertainty and source permissions.
- No public rankings from selected anecdotal cases or confidential internal experiments.

## Publication workflow
1. Draft a model-focused research brief using public or specially cleared source data.
2. Verify model IDs, experimental scope, rights, reproducibility and dates.
3. Pass confidentiality review for proprietary protocols, prompts, thresholds, benchmarks and incident details.
4. Obtain explicit founder approval of **the exact manuscript, charts and social copy**.
5. Release a versioned article and short platform-specific summaries referencing the same article.
6. Maintain corrections and updated model/version disclosures.

**Status:** Roadmap only. No proprietary or unpublished benchmark metrics are released by this file.
